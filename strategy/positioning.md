# Personal Direction and Positioning

Last updated: 2026-07-20

## Working positioning

> I help software teams improve how they build, document, and operate software using AI, automation, and modern engineering practices.

This combines engineering leadership with enterprise AI and leaves room for practical work in architecture, cloud infrastructure, DevOps, documentation, onboarding, and developer productivity.

## Audience

- Software engineering teams and their leaders.
- Small and mid-sized organizations that need pragmatic AI or engineering guidance.
- Developers interested in AI-enabled engineering practices and reliable delivery.

## Principles

- Sell expertise and leverage before selling more hours.
- Favor practical, secure, team-oriented AI use over novelty.
- Use public material to demonstrate thinking without exposing employer or client intellectual property.
- Build a body of useful work that can support consulting, products, speaking, and career opportunities over time.
