# Opportunity Backlog

This is a menu of possible income streams, not a commitment to pursue all of them. Prioritize based on customer evidence, personal energy, time-to-revenue, and long-term leverage.

## Highest-priority directions to validate

| Opportunity | Why it fits | Likely first validation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI consulting for engineering teams | Leverages AI, technical leadership, documentation, DevOps, cloud, and enterprise architecture experience. | Talk with prospective teams; define a paid AI-readiness assessment. |
| Focused AI SaaS | Offers long-term recurring-revenue potential around problems already understood firsthand. | Identify one narrowly defined, recurring pain point and interview users. |
| Digital products and templates | High leverage and low ongoing maintenance when based on proven working practices. | Publish one useful checklist, template, or playbook. |
| Technical writing and public content | Builds trust and creates demand for every other path. | Publish one high-quality article or project case study. |
| Fractional CTO / advisory work | Premium recurring work for organizations that need senior technical judgment part-time. | Define a compact advisory offer and referral-oriented outreach. |

## Additional ideas

- Fixed-price AI implementation packages: internal knowledge assistant, document automation, Confluence or SharePoint search, Teams/Slack assistant, or local/private AI deployment.
- Premium, highly specific technical courses: production AI agents, MCP servers, enterprise RAG, AI development workflows, Cloudflare for developers, or a team-lead playbook.
- Open-source developer tooling with paid hosted, enterprise, support, or consulting offerings.
- Specialist architecture, code, cloud, Docker, Terraform, or security reviews.
- Technical writing for architecture documents, developer documentation, white papers, and AI implementation guides.
- Resume, portfolio, promotion-package, or architecture-portfolio reviews for engineers.
- Educational video or newsletter content focused on AI-enabled engineering and leadership.

## Evaluation questions

Before committing to an idea, document:

1. Who has this problem and how urgently do they need it solved?
2. What proof do we have beyond personal intuition?
3. What is the smallest paid or observable experiment?
4. What would make this worth continuing after four to six weeks?
5. Are there confidentiality, conflict-of-interest, or employment-policy constraints?
